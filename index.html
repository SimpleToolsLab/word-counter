About Our Word Counter

This free online word counter helps you quickly calculate the number of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in your writing.

Your Privacy

Your text is processed directly inside your browser. We do not need to store the text you enter into the word counter.

How to Use the Word Counter

Using our free online word counter is simple. Type or paste your text into the text box and your word count, character count, sentence count and paragraph count will update automatically.

You can also upload a PDF, DOCX or TXT document to count the words directly from your file.

What Does the Word Counter Calculate?

The word counter analyzes your text and displays the total number of words, characters, sentences and paragraphs. It also estimates reading time and speaking time and shows the number of characters without spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this word counter free?

Yes. This online word counter is free to use and does not require an account.

Can I count characters as well as words?

Yes. The tool counts both characters and words, including a separate character count without spaces.

Can I upload a document?

Yes. You can upload PDF, DOCX and TXT files and the tool will extract the text for counting.

Is my text stored?

No. Your text is processed directly in your browser and is not uploaded to our server.
